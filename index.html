About

We know Jaguars.

DesignCats has been looking after Jaguars in the Andover area for many years. It started simply — a genuine love for these cars and a belief that their owners deserve a specialist who actually understands them.

People come here because they've tried elsewhere and wanted something better. Somewhere the mechanic speaks to you directly, tells you exactly what's happening with your car, and only does what's genuinely needed.

No unnecessary work. No upselling. Just honest, careful attention to every Jaguar that comes through the door — whether that's a routine service, a tricky diagnosis, or something more involved.

If you've been looking for someone who really knows these cars, you've found the right place.

What we do

Services

We cover the full range of Jaguar work, from a routine annual service to more involved mechanical and bodywork repairs. Everything is handled with the same level of care.

Servicing & Maintenance

Full and interim services carried out to manufacturer specification, using quality parts suited to your model and mileage.

Diagnostics

Specialist diagnostic equipment for all Jaguar systems. We find the real cause — not just the warning light. Then we explain it clearly.

Mechanical Repairs

Engine, gearbox, suspension, brakes — all mechanical work handled in-house by someone who has spent years working on these specific cars.

Bodywork & Paint

Careful bodywork and paint repairs to match the original finish. We take our time because the end result is what matters.

MOT Preparation

A thorough pre-MOT check to make sure there are no surprises on the day. We'll tell you exactly what needs attention — and what can wait.

Pre-Purchase Inspections

Thinking of buying a Jaguar? We'll look it over properly before you commit — the kind of thorough check that only a Jaguar specialist can do.
