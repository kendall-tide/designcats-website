What We Do

We cover the full range of work for Jaguar, Land Rover and other prestige and everyday vehicles. Everything handled with the same care and honesty.

Servicing & Maintenance

Regular servicing to manufacturer specification using quality parts. We'll tell you what needs doing and what can wait, and we won't add work that isn't necessary.

Repairs & Diagnostics

Thorough diagnosis of mechanical, electrical and electronic faults. We find the actual cause, explain it clearly, and only carry out work you've agreed to.

Restoration

Careful restoration of classic Jaguars and Land Rovers. We respect how these vehicles were built and take the time to get it right.

MOT Testing

Class IV MOT testing with an honest assessment of anything that needs attention. We'll advise on what's urgent and what can reasonably wait.

Pre-Purchase Inspection

Thinking of buying a Jaguar, Land Rover or other prestige car? A proper inspection before you commit can save you from an expensive mistake.

Parts Supply

We can source genuine, OEM and quality aftermarket parts for most makes and models, often at better prices than you'd find at a main dealer.
