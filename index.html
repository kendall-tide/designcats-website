What We Do

We cover the full range of Jaguar work, from routine servicing to restoration. Everything handled with the same care and honesty.

Servicing & Maintenance

Regular servicing to manufacturer specification using quality parts. We'll tell you what needs doing and what can wait, and we won't add work that isn't necessary.

Repairs & Diagnostics

Thorough diagnosis of mechanical, electrical and electronic faults. We find the actual cause, explain it clearly, and only carry out work you've agreed to.

Restoration

Careful restoration of classic Jaguars. We respect how these cars were built and take the time to get it right.

Remapping

ECU remapping tailored to your Jaguar, for better performance, smoother power delivery or improved economy. We'll talk through what's realistic for your car before any work starts.

Pre-Purchase Inspection

Thinking of buying a Jaguar? A proper inspection before you commit can save you from an expensive mistake.

Parts Supply

We can source genuine, OEM and quality aftermarket Jaguar parts, often at better prices than you'd find at a main dealer.

Recovery for our customers

If your Jaguar lets you down, existing customers can call us and we'll arrange to recover it back to the workshop.
