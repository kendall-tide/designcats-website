DesignCats — Image Guide
========================

Place your images in this folder, then follow the swap-in notes
in index.html (look for the HTML comments).

Recommended images:

  hero.jpg
  --------
  A high-quality photo of a Jaguar in or near the workshop.
  Landscape orientation. Minimum 1600px wide.
  Something calm and confident — not a showroom shot.
  The dark overlay in CSS will tone it down appropriately.

  workshop.jpg
  ------------
  A photo of the workshop or the owner at work.
  Can be portrait or landscape — the CSS will crop it to fill.
  Something that shows craft and care rather than a posed shot.

  map.jpg (optional)
  ------------------
  A static map screenshot, or replace the placeholder div with
  a Google Maps iframe — see the comment in index.html.

Swapping in hero.jpg:
  In index.html, inside .hero__bg, remove the <div class="hero__image-placeholder"> line
  and replace with: <img src="images/hero.jpg" alt="Jaguar in the DesignCats workshop">
  Then in style.css, update .hero__bg to:
    position: relative; width: 100%; height: 100%;
  and add:
    .hero__bg img { width: 100%; height: 100%; object-fit: cover; object-position: center; }

Swapping in workshop.jpg:
  In index.html, inside .about__image, remove the .image-placeholder div
  and replace with: <img src="images/workshop.jpg" alt="The DesignCats workshop">

File format notes:
  - JPG is fine for photos (quality 80–85 is plenty)
  - Keep hero under 400KB, workshop under 200KB for fast loading
  - WebP is supported in all modern browsers and will be smaller if your
    editing tool can export it — just change the file extension in index.html
